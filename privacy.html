Aura Check

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Aura Check app (“Aura Check”, “we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We built Aura Check to collect as little as possible: there’s no sign-up, no ads, and no tracking.

The short version
  • You don’t create an account. The app uses a random, anonymous ID.
  • When you scan, your photo is sent to our AI provider to identify the bottle. We don’t store your photos on our servers.
  • Your scan history, Rotation, prices and wear log stay on your phone.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your data anytime in Settings → Delete My Data.

1. Information we collect

Photos you scan. When you take or choose a photo to scan, the app makes a smaller copy and sends it to our server, which passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to recognize the fragrance. The photo is used only to identify the bottle. Our server does not save it. Before your first scan, the app asks for your permission to do this.

Anonymous ID. The first time you scan, the app creates a random anonymous account ID with our backend provider. It isn’t linked to your name, email, phone number, or Apple ID.

Scan records. For each successful scan we record your anonymous ID and the time of the scan, so we can count free scans.

Fragrance results. We save the fragrance information the AI returns (for example the name, brand, notes and ratings) so the same bottle always shows the same result. These saved results are not linked to you or your anonymous ID.

Purchases. If you subscribe to Aura Check Pro, Apple processes your payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase details and your anonymous ID. We never receive your payment card details.

Information that stays on your phone. Your scan history, saved photos, Rotation, prices paid, and wear log are stored only on your device. We don’t receive them.

What we don’t collect. We don’t collect your name, email address (unless you email us), contacts, location, or advertising identifiers, and we don’t use analytics or advertising tools.

2. How we use information

  • To identify the fragrance in your photo and show you its details, aura score and dupes.
  • To count free scans and manage your subscription.
  • To keep the service working, secure, and free of abuse.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Aura Check, and only as needed:

  • Anthropic (AI provider): receives the photo you scan to identify the fragrance. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models.
  • Supabase (backend hosting): runs our server and stores your anonymous ID and scan records.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: process and manage subscriptions.

We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Links to other websites

“Shop” buttons open other websites, such as stores. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We may earn a commission from links in this app.

5. How long we keep information

Scan records are kept while your anonymous account exists. Photos are not kept on our servers. Data on your phone stays there until you delete it or delete the app.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete My Data to permanently delete your scan records and anonymous account from our server and erase everything Aura Check stored on your phone. Deleting the app erases the data on your phone, but your anonymous scan records stay on our server, so use Delete My Data first if you want those removed. You can also email us to ask about or delete your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information. Contact us and we’ll help.

7. Children

Aura Check is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we’ll delete any related data.

8. Security and storage

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our providers, whose servers may be located in the United States. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We’ll change the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests: auracheck.app@gmail.com
